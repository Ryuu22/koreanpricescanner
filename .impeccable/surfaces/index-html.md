# Surface: card catalog (whole site shell)

Scope: index.html (catalog + card popup) and the shared shell every page uses: generated card/set/products/movers pages, calc.html, about.html, privacy.html. Mode: Operate (Read for about/privacy).

Audience and job: Korean Riftbound traders checking a price mid-trade on a phone; collectors tracking high-end cards and sealed product. Task: find a card, read one trustworthy number, see how it was made. Must keep: card art as the hero, mobile bottom tab bar and filter sheet, Korean red-up / blue-down. Must not feel like: a crypto/fintech dashboard, a generic shop template, or a sparse slow-to-scan page.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The catalog is a Korean card shop's glass showcase: every card sits in its toploader with a white printed price sticker. It refuses both the dark gamer card database and the white marketplace grid.

OWN-WORLD: Showcase-felt charcoal ground; cards framed in clear toploader sleeves (translucent frame, heavier top lip); white sticker labels in near-black ink for every price; glass shelf rules between rows as flat lighter bands; red and blue only for price movement; the active state looks like a sticker (white on charcoal). Pretendard, tabular figures.

STORY: The visitor recognises the shop wall, reads the sticker, trusts it because the sticker says how it was priced (median, listings, updated), and taps through to listings.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim charcoal top bar (name, EN), pinned search with filter button, then two columns of toploadered cards on shelves, each with a sticker: name, big price, code and listing count; bottom tab bar.

FORM: Card Shop Showcase, position 1 on my ordered list (picked by the user over the rolled position 7). seed:a355f99eb5ea8b42. Raises kept: tabular digit-aligned prices; totals right-aligned tight; swipe to the neighbouring card in the popup; every control state drawn; flat fills, no gradients or glows.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
